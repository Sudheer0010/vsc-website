# VSC Creative Brief

## Objective
To establish VSC Capital & Advisory's digital platform as the most thoughtfully designed research environment in Indian finance — an institutional headquarters where typography, whitespace, motion, and interaction reflect the same disciplined mindset that underpins the investment process.

## Target Audience & Visitor Psychology
1. **Visitor C (Primary / Highest Priority)**: Investor with capital seeking a disciplined process. Asking: *"Why should I trust VSC's research and decision-making framework?"*
2. **Visitor A**: First-time discoverer. Asking: *"What makes VSC different from retail brokerages or finfluencer channels?"*
3. **Visitor B**: Returning research reader. Accessing market letters, research library, and methodology updates.

## Benchmark Peer Group
- *Bridgewater Associates* (Institutional research & economic memos)
- *BlackRock Research Institute* (Calm, structured capital reports)
- *Morningstar Publications* (Editorial data presentation)
- *Howard Marks Memos* (Thoughtful, long-term investment philosophy)
- *McKinsey Reports* (High typographic hierarchy & clear visual structure)
