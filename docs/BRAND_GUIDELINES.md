# VSC Brand Guidelines

## 1. Visual Language
- **Typography Carries the Design**: Large display headlines (*Cormorant Garamond*), clean monospaced subtext (*DM Mono*), confident sans-serif UI typography (*Syne* / *Geist*).
- **Breathing Room**: Generous vertical and horizontal whitespace (80px–140px section padding).
- **Curated Color Palette**:
  - **Deep Navy Base**: `#060810` (Primary background), `#0B0F1E` (Secondary elevated panels)
  - **Warm Gold Accent**: `#C9A84C` (Emphasis, active indicators, selective CTAs)
  - **Off-White Text**: `#F4F1EC` (Primary headings & high contrast copy)
  - **Muted Slate Text**: `#A7A7AD` (Secondary body copy & metadata)
- **Zero Decorative Noise**: Avoid generic boxy card grids, heavy gradients, dashboard widgets, and speculative chart graphics.

## 2. Copywriting & Tone
- **Calm, Intelligent, Process-Driven**: Use direct, measured statements.
- **Never Salesy**: No "Buy Now", "Act Fast", "Unlock Returns", or "Limited Spots".
- **Focus on Discipline**: Capital protection, cash as a position, waiting as a decision, evidence over opinions.
