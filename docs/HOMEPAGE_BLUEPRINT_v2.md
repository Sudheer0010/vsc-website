# Homepage Blueprint v2 — The Cognitive Journey Wireframe

> **Mindset**: The current homepage is treated as incorrect.  
> **Source of Truth**: This Frozen Blueprint v2 dictates the exact order, reading budget, word count caps, and primary takeaways.  
> **Total Information Budget**: **Under 110 seconds (< 2 minutes)** total reading time.

---

## Cognitive Module Sequence

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Module 1: Arrival} &\longrightarrow \text{First Impression (20s Budget)} \\
\text{Module 2: Problem} &\longrightarrow \text{Why VSC Exists (20s Budget)} \\

## 1. Module 1: Arrival

- **Cognitive Purpose**: Establish immediate institutional authority and clarity.
- **Maximum Reading Time**: **20 seconds**.
- **Maximum Word Count**: **45 words**.
- **Primary Message**:
  - *Header Tag*: `WELCOME TO VSC CAPITAL & ADVISORY`
  - *Headline*: **A Smarter Way to Build and Protect Capital.**
  - *Subtext*: We study market structure, trend strength, and risk management to deploy capital with systematic discipline.
  - *Primary Action*: `Our Investment Framework` $\rightarrow$ `/offerings`
  - *Secondary Action*: `Read Our Thinking` $\rightarrow$ `/blog`
- **Dominant Visual**: High-contrast research desk photo (`/vsc_annual_report_hero.png`).
- **Single Takeaway**: *"VSC is an institutional firm focused on protecting capital with systematic discipline."*

---

## 2. Module 2: Problem

- **Cognitive Purpose**: Establish why VSC exists by highlighting the core problem in retail finance.
- **Maximum Reading Time**: **20 seconds**.
- **Maximum Word Count**: **55 words**.
- **Primary Message**:
  - *Header Tag*: `THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE`
  - *Headline*: **Information Exploded. Understanding Didn't.**
  - *Body*: Retail investors do not fail from a lack of financial news—they fail from trading without a systematic process. VSC exists as an institutional research desk where cash is an active position and capital preservation precedes compounding.
- **Single Takeaway**: *"VSC solves the problem of emotional trading by providing a systematic institutional process."*

---

## 3. Module 3: Belief

- **Cognitive Purpose**: Present the four core pillars governing VSC's investment mindset.
- **Maximum Reading Time**: **30 seconds**.
- **Maximum Word Count**: **70 words**.
- **Primary Message**:
  - *Header Tag*: `HOW WE THINK`
  - *Headline*: **Capital Protection Precedes Compounding.**
  - *The 4 Pillars (Annual Report Numbering)*:
    - **`01` Protect Capital First**: *Surviving drawdown cycles is the precondition for compounding.*
    - **`02` Respect the Process**: *Quantitative rules outperform emotional discretion.*
    - **`03` Patience Compounds**: *Waiting in cash during uncompensated regimes is an active decision.*
    - **`04` Continuous Improvement**: *Refining systematic risk filters as microstructures evolve.*
- **Single Takeaway**: *"VSC operates by four strict principles: protection, process, patience, and improvement."*

---

## 4. Module 4: Proof

- **Cognitive Purpose**: Provide instant, 10-second visual proof of why VSC operates differently from traditional mutual funds.
- **Maximum Reading Time**: **15 seconds**.
- **Maximum Word Count**: **50 words**.
- **Primary Message**:
  - *Header Tag*: `THE PROOF OF DIFFERENCE`
  - *Headline*: **Mutual Funds Stay Invested by Mandate. VSC Deploys Capital by Discipline.**
  - *Micro-Contrasts (McKinsey Style)*:
    - **Stays invested** (100% of time regardless of crash risk) $\longleftrightarrow$ **Moves to cash** (whenever risk is uncompensated).
    - **Tracks the benchmark** (chasing relative returns and fee targets) $\longleftrightarrow$ **Tracks the risk** (reading trend strength and quantitative signals).
    - **Rides drawdowns out** (no defensive exit rules, no floor) $\longleftrightarrow$ **Cuts risk early** (automated stop logic, position reduction triggers).
  - *Closing Verdict*: *"One mandate is built to stay in. The other is built to know when to leave."*
- **Single Takeaway**: *"Traditional funds are forced to stay in; VSC knows when to move to cash."*

---

## 5. Module 5: Evidence

- **Cognitive Purpose**: Provide proof that VSC actually publishes high-conviction research.
- **Maximum Reading Time**: **15 seconds**.
- **Maximum Word Count**: **35 words**.
- **Primary Message**:
  - *Header Tag*: `RESEARCH EVIDENTIARY PROOF`
  - *Headline*: **We Do Not Publish News. We Publish Thinking.**
  - *Single Featured Teaser*:

    - *Action*: `Read Latest Market Letter →` $\rightarrow$ `/blog`

---

## 6. Module 6: Action

- **Cognitive Purpose**: Provide a single reflection question and an unhurried, dignified exit to initiate a conversation.
- **Maximum Reading Time**: **10 seconds**.
- **Maximum Word Count**: **25 words**.
- **Primary Message**:
  - *Single Reflection Question*: *"Is activity improving your returns—or just your comfort?"*
  - *Header Tag*: `BEGIN THE CONVERSATION`
  - *Headline*: **Serious Investing Begins With Serious Conversation.**
  - *Action Button*: `Schedule a Strategic Discussion →` $\rightarrow$ `/enquire`
- **Single Takeaway**: *"Initiating a strategic discussion with VSC is the next logical step."*
