# VSC Homepage Wireframe Specification (Phase III Specs)

> **Narrative Flow**: The Digital Headquarters opens with an uninterrupted research document arc. Zero rigid section cuts.

---

## Storyboard Architecture

```
[R-01 ARRIVAL] ──> [R-02 THESIS] ──> [R-03 METHODOLOGY] ──> [R-04 JOURNAL] ──> [R-05 FRAMEWORK] ──> [R-06 INVITATION]
```

### Document R-01: ARRIVAL (00s - 30s)
- **Tag**: `R-01 / ARRIVAL / 2026`
- **Ritual**: Research Line draws.
- **Thesis**: *"A Smarter Way to Build and Protect Capital. Invest with Process. Not Predictions."*
- **Layout**: Split editorial view: serif headline on left; art-directed research desk photography on right.
- **Reflection Exit**: *"What if investing began with protecting capital?"*

---

### Document R-02: THESIS & MANIFESTO (30s - 60s)
- **Tag**: `R-02 / THESIS / 2026`
- **Ritual**: Vertical Gold Thread draws.
- **Thesis**: *"Information exploded. Understanding didn't. Everyone has opinions. Few have a process."*
- **Layout**: Asymmetrical 2-column publication block with academic margin notes.
- **Reflection Exit**: *"When market noise increases, what anchors your decisions?"*

---

### Document R-03: METHODOLOGY (60s - 90s)
- **Tag**: `R-03 / METHODOLOGY / 2026`
- **Ritual**: Research Line draws.
- **Thesis**: *"Capital protection is not the absence of risk. It is the disciplined management of it."*
- **Layout**: Four core beliefs with monospaced indices (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`).
- **Reflection Exit**: *"If cash is a position, how often do you choose discipline over activity?"*

---

### Document R-04: MARKET LETTERS (90s - 120s)
- **Tag**: `R-04 / JOURNAL / 2026`
- **Ritual**: Date stamp + Research Line.
- **Thesis**: *"We do not publish news. We publish thinking."*
- **Layout**: Modeled after *Financial Times Weekend* — publication date rule, serif headlines, drop caps.
- **Reflection Exit**: *"What assumptions is the market making today?"*

---

### Document R-05: FRAMEWORK (120s - 150s)
- **Tag**: `R-05 / FRAMEWORK / 2026`
- **Ritual**: Research Line draws.
- **Thesis**: *"Mutual Funds stay fully invested by mandate. VSC deploys capital by discipline."*
- **Layout**: Institutional briefing matrix contrasting Mutual Funds vs. VSC.
- **Reflection Exit**: *"Is activity improving your returns—or just your comfort?"*

---

### Document R-06: INVITATION (150s+)
- **Tag**: `R-06 / INVITATION / 2026`
- **Ritual**: Vertical Gold Thread draws.
- **Thesis**: *"Every investor remembers the moment they decided to take investing seriously."*
- **Layout**: Strategic Discussion Panel with compliance disclaimers.
- **Reflection Exit**: *"What decision are you trying to make?"*
