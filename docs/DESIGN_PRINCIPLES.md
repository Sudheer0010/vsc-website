# VSC Design Principles

1. **Content Over Decoration**: Typography outranks graphics. Whitespace outranks effects. Research outranks marketing.
2. **Institutional Calm**: Never induce urgency or FOMO. Reflect the calm of a serious research environment.
3. **Selective Gold**: Warm gold (`#C9A84C`) represents intentional emphasis. It must never dominate a view.
4. **Editorial Rhythm**: Layouts should read like a premium publication (*Financial Times*, *Morningstar Report*, *Harvard Business Review*).
