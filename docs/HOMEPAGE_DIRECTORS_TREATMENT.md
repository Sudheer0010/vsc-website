# Phase III — Homepage Director's Treatment

> **Mindset Shift**: The homepage is not a composition of UI widgets. It is **the opening chapter of an institutional research platform**.  
> **Core Objective**: Optimizing for narrative coherence and emotional trust — not visual novelty.

## 2. Emotional Guardrails (What the Visitor Must Never Feel)

- ❌ **Never Sold To**: Zero sales pitches, limited spots, or retail buy buttons.
- ❌ **Never Rushed**: No countdown timers, FOMO triggers, or urgent CTAs.
- ❌ **Never Overwhelmed**: No chaotic dashboard widgets or complex charts.
- ❌ **Never Entertained**: No gratuitous animations, particle effects, or 3D gimmicks.
- ❌ **Never Impressed by Effects**: Motion exists strictly in service of reading tempo.
- ❌ **Never Confused**: Clear, measured, declarative typography.

### Target Emotional State
$$\text{Curious} \quad \vert \quad \text{Calm} \quad \vert \quad \text{Informed} \quad \vert \quad \text{Respected} \quad \vert \quad \text{Thoughtful} \quad \vert \quad \text{Confident}$$

\text{R-03 METHODOLOGY} &\longrightarrow \text{How does VSC think?} \\
\text{R-04 MARKET LETTERS} &\longrightarrow \text{How does VSC demonstrate that thinking?} \\
\text{R-05 FRAMEWORK} &\longrightarrow \text{How is that thinking applied?} \\
\text{R-06 INVITATION} &\longrightarrow \text{How can I engage?}
\end{aligned}$$

---

## 4. Digital Experience vs. Print Metaphor Rule

- **Borrow Print Discipline**: Large serif typography, academic margin notes, drop-cap metadata, line length constraints (65–75 chars).
- **Avoid Over-Literal Mimicry**: Do NOT make the site look like a scanned vintage newspaper or old paper texture background. It remains a clean, high-performance, unmistakably digital dark-mode interface (`#060810`).

---

## 5. The 20% Reduction Rule

After composing `app/page.tsx`, evaluate every line and block. Remove 20% of redundant copy and container padding so that the remaining narrative speaks with quiet, unhurried authority.
