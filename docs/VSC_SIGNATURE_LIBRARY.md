# VSC Signature Library

> **Brand Recognition Directive**: These 7 signatures render VSC instantly recognizable, even if the logo mark is removed.

---

## Signature 01: The Research Line
- **Description**: A single hairline gold rule (`#C9A84C`) that draws itself horizontally before every research document.
- **Symbolism**: *Research begins.*
- **Choreography**: Draws left-to-right over 0.8s (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).

---

## Signature 02: Research Document Identifiers
- **Description**: Monospaced coordinate tags framing each chapter.
- **Format**: `R-01 / ARRIVAL / 2026`, `R-02 / THESIS / 2026`, `R-03 / METHODOLOGY / 2026`, `R-04 / JOURNAL / 2026`, `R-05 / FRAMEWORK / 2026`, `R-06 / INVITATION / 2026`.
- **Typography**: *DM Mono*, 10px–11px, 0.2em letter-spacing, uppercase.

---

## Signature 03: Reflection Questions
- **Description**: Intellectual reflection questions framing page conclusions.
- **Questions**:
  - **Home**: *"What if investing began with protecting capital?"*
  - **Market Letters**: *"What assumptions is the market making today?"*
  - **Framework**: *"Is activity improving your returns—or just your comfort?"*
  - **About**: *"Why does process matter more than prediction?"*
  - **Discussion**: *"What decision are you trying to make?"*

---

## Signature 04: Academic Margin Notes
- **Description**: Editorial side annotations set in the right margin, mirroring academic publications and research journals.
- **Typography**: *DM Mono*, 11px, slate muted text, left-bordered by hairline rule.

---

## Signature 05: Background Research Grid
- **Description**: Barely visible 16px background research grid pattern over matte surfaces.
- **Specification**: `radial-gradient(rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.015) 1px, transparent 1px)` with `16px 16px` size.

---

## Signature 06: Vertical Gold Thread
- **Description**: A subtle vertical 1px gold rule along the left margin connecting narrative chapters.
- **Symbolism**: The uninterrupted thread of disciplined process guiding the reader down the page.

---

## Signature 07: Publication Metadata Rules
- **Description**: Date stamps, issue numbers, drop caps, and author byline rules inspired by *Financial Times Weekend*.
- **Usage**: Used in Market Letters, briefing matrices, and research reports.
