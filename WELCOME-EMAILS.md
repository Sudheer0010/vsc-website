## Notes for setup

- All three emails plain text or minimal HTML. No images, no headers,
  no coloured buttons, no banner. They should look like a person wrote
  them, because a person did.

- "From" name should be "Sudheer at VSC Capital" — not "VSC Capital &
  Advisory" and not "noreply@". Personal sender increases open rate and
  makes the day-8 reply feel natural.

- Email 3 is the most important email in the entire system. Replies to
  it: (a) dramatically improve deliverability, (b) start real
  conversations, (c) feed the Product Signals file. Read and respond
  to every reply personally through at least 1,000 subscribers.

- Day 3 and Day 8 spacing is deliberate. Day 3 gives the framework
  while the signup is still fresh. Day 8 is far enough that the
  question doesn't feel like an immediate sales follow-up.

- After email 3, the subscriber enters the regular cadence: research
  notes as published, market letter monthly. No further automated
  sequences until 1,000+ subscribers.
